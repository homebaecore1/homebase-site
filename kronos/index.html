Kronos

Calendar integration for Homebase.

Kronos is Homebase's calendar and scheduling module. It aggregates calendar information into the locally operated Homebase platform so that scheduling information can be used alongside other Homebase capabilities.

Google Calendar integration

When authorised by the user, Kronos connects to Google Calendar using read-only access.

Kronos may retrieve calendar lists and calendar event information required to display and synchronise the user's calendars within Homebase.

Kronos does not use its Google Calendar integration to create, modify or delete events in Google Calendar.

Google Calendar data imported by Kronos is stored locally within the user's Homebase installation. Google OAuth credentials used by the integration are also stored locally and protected using Windows credential encryption mechanisms.

Access can be revoked by the user through their Google Account permissions.
