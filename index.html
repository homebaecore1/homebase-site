Homebase

Personal computing, automation and decision support.

Homebase is a privately developed personal computing platform designed to bring local automation, scheduling, environmental control and decision-support capabilities into a modular system.

Homebase is designed around user control, modularity and local ownership of data. Individual modules provide specific capabilities while remaining separated by defined interfaces and permission boundaries.

Kronos

Kronos is the calendar module within Homebase. It provides calendar aggregation and scheduling capabilities and can connect to supported external calendar providers, including Google Calendar.

Google Calendar integration is read-only. Imported Google Calendar information is used to provide calendar information within the user's locally operated Homebase system.
